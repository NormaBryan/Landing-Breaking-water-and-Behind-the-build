Task 1 — Think

Finding the Opportunity

My original hunch before using AI

“It could be used for different outdoor activities that are seasonal or weather dependant like horse riding, Kayaking or boat trips.”

The problem

The case business was Atlantic Edge Surf School in Lahinch. Weather cancellations created extra administration because customers had to be contacted and rebooked.

The school had six sessions per day, twelve places per session and five instructors. In the case, weather cancellations occurred on approximately one in four days. A cancelled day could involve around three hours of manual rebooking work, and approximately one in five cancelled bookings was never rebooked.

My chosen niche and buyer

I decided to focus on independent Irish surf schools, particularly along the Wild Atlantic Way. The intended buyer is the surf school owner or manager responsible for lessons, instructors and bookings.

Customer statements

I used illustrative customer statements to show the owner's problem. These were invented examples, not actual customer interviews.

Competitor research

I considered existing booking platforms including eola and SimplyBook.me . Breakwater focuses specifically on weather cancellations and rebooking rather than replacing every booking system.

Product hypothesis

If independent surf schools can quickly identify affected bookings, find suitable new sessions and see income at risk, they may spend less time chasing customers after cancellations.

Proposed pricing

€99 one-off setup and €49 per month. These prices are assumptions for the student project and have not been validated with customers.

Task 2 — Build

Building and Testing the Alpha

What I built

I used AI to help create a mobile-first prototype in one HTML file containing HTML, CSS and JavaScript. It used fourteen fictional sample bookings and four views:

  • Today: View lessons and cancel sessions.
  • Rebook: See customers waiting for another session.
  • Money: View potential booking income at risk.
  • Calendar: View future sessions and available places.

The value at risk was calculated using €45 per person. The rebooking queue used amber after 24 hours and red after 48 hours.

Alpha test — What I tried

I tested the prototype on my computer by using the cancellation and rebooking functions and checking that the Money view updated.

What broke or confused me

Initially, the cancellation button did not work correctly. I also found that the Calendar view was missing from the first version.

What I changed

I asked AI to correct the cancellation function and add the Calendar view. I then checked the updated version on my computer and confirmed the functions were working.

Phone test

After fixing the prototype on my computer, I downloaded and opened it on my phone, where it worked. I did not separately record a full phone test of every capacity rule or a browser refresh persistence test.

What I learned

AI helped me build the prototype quickly, but I still needed to test the buttons and check that the application worked as intended. The first version was not perfect, and testing helped me identify what needed to change.

Task 3 — Sell

Creating the Landing Page

Product name and promise

Breakwater — Less chasing. More surfing. I wanted the headline to explain the benefit immediately to a surf school owner.

How I presented the product

The landing page explains weather cancellations and presents the product in three simple steps: cancel a lesson, find another suitable slot, and track customers and income at risk.

Calls to action

I included a Try the Live Demo button that opens my published Task 2 prototype. I also included a demonstration waiting-list form. The form does not send, save or collect email addresses; it only displays a thank-you message.

Pricing

I displayed a proposed €49 monthly price with a €99 one-off setup fee. This is illustrative pricing, not a confirmed commercial offer.

Design decisions

I used a dark navy and sea-green colour scheme to connect the product visually with surfing and the Atlantic coast. I designed the page for mobile screens first because a surf school owner may check bookings while working outdoors.

Limitations

Breakwater is an alpha student-project prototype, not a fully launched commercial product. The customer statements, prices and potential benefits have not been validated through real customer trials.

What I learned

This task helped me understand the difference between building a working prototype and explaining why someone might want to use it. The landing page needed to focus on the owner's problem rather than simply listing technology features.
